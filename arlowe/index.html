FAQ

How do the pieces fit?

The sweatshirt and overshirt have a relaxed, straight fit; the jeans have a wide leg. If you are unsure, our customer service team is happy to help.

How do I care for the print?

Ideally wash inside out at 30 °C, do not bleach and do not tumble dry. Do not iron over the print.

What are the pieces made of?

The sweatshirt, jeans and overshirt are made of 100 % cotton.

Where are the items available?

In selected ARLOWE stores. Availability may vary by store – see Service & contact for details.

About ARLOWE

ARLOWE stands for timeless basics with a signature style: soft materials, clean cuts and prints that show personality without being loud.

Our collection is designed for young adults looking for clothes that are comfortable and easy to combine.

Sustainability

We continuously review our materials, supply chains and packaging to conserve resources. Cotton is a natural raw material that can be worn for a long time when cared for well.
