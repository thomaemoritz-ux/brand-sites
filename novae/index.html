The serum

Light in texture. Intense in care.

The Radiant Serum combines a fine, fast-absorbing texture with a formula that hydrates the skin and leaves it feeling supple. It is the heart of every routine – and the step you notice most.

Ingredients

Few ingredients. Clearly formulated.

How to use

Three steps. Every day.

  1. 1

    Cleanse

    Gently cleanse your face and pat dry.

  2. 2

    Apply

    Spread a few drops of serum over face, neck and décolleté.

  3. 3

    Pat in

    Gently pat in until the serum is fully absorbed. Then apply the Renewing Cream.

FAQ

How often should I use the Radiant Serum?

Morning and evening after cleansing. Spread a few drops over face, neck and décolleté, pat in gently, then apply the Renewing Cream.

Which skin types is the serum suitable for?

The serum is designed for daily care. If you have very sensitive skin, we recommend testing it on a small area first.

Where can I find all ingredients?

The full ingredient list (INCI) is printed on the packaging. We introduce the key ingredients in the Ingredients section.

How does the serum fit into my routine?

It is the third step: first the Gentle Cleanser, then the Balancing Toner, followed by the Radiant Serum and finally the Renewing Cream.
