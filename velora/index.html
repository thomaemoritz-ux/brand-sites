Made for every journey.

Technology

Performance that feels effortless.

  1. 01

    Electric all-wheel drive

    One electric motor on each axle distributes power as conditions require. The result is confident traction in any weather and instant acceleration.

  2. 02

    Intelligent energy management

    Battery preconditioning, efficient thermal management and regenerative braking work together to keep range and charging performance reliable every day.

  3. 03

    Driver assistance systems

    Assistance systems help with lane keeping, distance control and parking and take the strain out of long journeys. The driver always remains in charge.

Driving

Dynamics that feel natural.

The V7 delivers its power instantly, without delay and without noise. The chassis combines comfort with precision, and the cabin stays quiet – on city streets just as on a long coastal drive.

FAQ

How far can the V7 go on a single charge?

The V7 offers a range of up to 610 km. The actual range depends on driving style, temperature, route profile and load.

How fast can the V7 be charged?

At a DC fast charger you go from 10 to 80 % in around 28 minutes. At home you charge via a wallbox, for example overnight.

Which driver assistance systems are available?

The V7 supports you with lane keeping, distance control and parking. The systems make long journeys easier; the driver always remains in charge.

About VELORA

VELORA Motors develops all-electric vehicles for everyday life and long distances: quiet, clear and efficient. We focus on premium materials, intuitive controls and technology that concentrates on the essentials.

The VELORA V7 is our first model, an all-electric premium SUV that combines comfort, efficiency and confidence.

Sustainability
